Sales by hour of day

Average sales generated in each clock-hour on a typical day (total for that hour ÷ 31 days). The line shows average number of orders, so you can see where the queue forms.

The day has a clear twin-peak shape: a long breakfast build from 7–10 AM, then a sharp 1 PM lunch spike (~₹13.7k/day) that alone is bigger than the entire dinner service. After 3 PM trade tapers steadily; past 8 PM it's negligible.

Share of sales by meal period

Orders bucketed by time of day: Breakfast 6–11 AM · Lunch 11 AM–3 PM · Tea/Snacks 3–7 PM · Dinner after 7 PM.

Lunch is the business — half of all revenue (₹10.4L) and the richest basket (₹113 avg bill). Breakfast brings the most footfall but cheaper tickets. Dinner is barely 4% — worth deciding whether it earns its keep.
